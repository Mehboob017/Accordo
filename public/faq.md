## 1. About Shree Dhootapapeshwar Limited

### Q1. What is Shree Dhootapapeshwar Limited?

Shree Dhootapapeshwar Limited (SDL) is an Indian Ayurvedic medicine company with over 150 years of heritage in Ayurved. The company follows the principles of Saiddhantik Ayurved and offers classical and other Ayurvedic formulations.
Source:
https://www.sdlindia.com/faqs
### Q2. When was Shree Dhootapapeshwar established?
Shree Dhootapapeshwar traces its establishment to 1872.
The establishment year represents the company's historical origin and should not be confused with its legal incorporation date.
Source:
https://www.sdlindia.com/about-us/company-profile
### Q3. How long has SDL been associated with Ayurved?

Shree Dhootapapeshwar has a legacy spanning more than 150 years.
Its website describes a continuing commitment to traditional Ayurvedic knowledge, medicine manufacturing, quality, and standardisation.
Source:
https://www.sdlindia.com/faqs
### Q4. Does SDL manufacture Ayurvedic medicines?
Yes. SDL manufactures Ayurvedic medicines and offers a portfolio that includes more than 350 classical Ayurvedic medicines, alongside other Ayurvedic formulations.
Individual product pages should be consulted for product-specific manufacturer information.
Source:
https://www.sdlindia.com/faqs
### Q5. What is Saiddhantik Ayurved?
Saiddhantik Ayurved is the Ayurvedic philosophy identified by SDL as a foundation of its approach to developing and manufacturing Ayurvedic formulations.
For company-specific information about this philosophy, consult SDL's official company profile.
Source:
https://www.sdlindia.com/about-us/company-profile
### Q6. What are Shree Dhootapapeshwar Standards?
Shree Dhootapapeshwar Standards (SDS) represent SDL's approach to defining and evaluating quality parameters for its Ayurvedic formulations.
These standards support the evaluation of formulation identity, quality, and consistency.
Source:
https://www.sdlindia.com/faqs

## 5. Shipping and Delivery
### Q23. What shipping charges apply to consumer orders?
SDL states that consumer orders with a net value below INR 600 incur a delivery charge of INR 60.
Consumer orders with a net value of INR 600 or above qualify for free shipping across India.
Source:
https://www.sdlindia.com/faqs
### Q24. What shipping charges apply to doctor orders?
SDL states that doctor orders with a net value below INR 2,000 incur a delivery charge of INR 150.
Doctor orders with a net value of INR 2,000 or above qualify for free shipping across India.
Source:
https://www.sdlindia.com/faqs
### Q25. Does SDL India provide free shipping?
Yes, subject to the stated order-value thresholds.
Consumer orders:
Free shipping at INR 600 or above.
Doctor orders:
Free shipping at INR 2,000 or above.

Source:
https://www.sdlindia.com/faqs
### Q26. Does SDL ship Ayurvedic products across India?
SDL's FAQ states that qualifying consumer and doctor orders receive free shipping across India.
For actual delivery eligibility and availability, customers should check their order details during checkout.
Source:
https://www.sdlindia.com/faqs
