/* ABOUT THE ORGANIZATION */

Shree Dhootapapeshwar Limited is an Indian Ayurvedic medicine company established in 1872.  The company manufactures classical Ayurvedic medicines and Ayurvedic formulations. SDL's portfolio includes more than 350 classical Ayurvedic medicines.

The company follows its philosophy of Saiddhantik Ayurved and maintains its own Shree Dhootapapeshwar    Standards (SDS).
