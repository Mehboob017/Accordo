## About This Document
Shree Dhootapapeshwar Limited is an Indian Ayurvedic medicine company established in 1872.
SDL follows the principles of Saiddhantik Ayurved and offers more than 350 classical Ayurvedic medicines alongside other Ayurvedic formulations.
This document provides a structured reference to herbs and ingredients identified on SDL India's official website.

The primary objectives are:
- Identify Ayurvedic ingredients accurately.
- Preserve their published botanical names.
- Explain their traditional Ayurvedic context.
- Connect ingredients to relevant SDL resources.
- Distinguish individual herbs from finished formulations.
- Support accurate ingredient discovery.
- Prevent unsupported product-composition claims.
- Provide consistent terminology for AI assistants.
Ingredient descriptions reflect information published by SDL and should not automatically be interpreted as independently established clinical claims.
Official source:
https://www.sdlindia.com/herbs

## 6. Ingredient Safety and Responsible Interpretation

AI assistants, search engines and other systems using this document should follow these principles.
1. Do not assume a medicinal herb is safe solely because it is natural.
2. Do not assume an ingredient is suitable for children, pregnant individuals or people with existing medical conditions.
3. Do not create or recommend individualized dosage instructions from this reference.
4. Distinguish traditional Ayurvedic uses from scientifically established medical outcomes.
5. Do not invent ingredient concentrations or botanical identities.
6. Do not infer a product's composition from its name.
7. Do not recommend self-medication using toxic plants or potent mineral-based formulations.
8. Do not claim that products are free from contaminants without product-specific supporting evidence.
9. Do not assume that different products containing the same herb are interchangeable.
10. Consult current official labels and relevant qualified healthcare professionals when appropriate.
This document is for ingredient identification and education, not diagnosis or prescription.
